# Trademark Policy

The Hexmark source code is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).
The **name "Hexmark" and the Hexmark logo** are not. They identify the official
project maintained by Hexium Studio, and section 6 of the Apache License
explicitly grants no rights to use them.

This policy explains what you may and may not do with the name and logo.

## You may

- Use the name "Hexmark" to refer to the project truthfully, for example
  "runs on Hexmark", "a plugin for Hexmark" or "compatible with Hexmark".
- Run unmodified official releases, for yourself or others, and call them Hexmark.
- Fork the code, modify it and redistribute it under the terms of the license.
- Keep the default "Powered by Hexmark" footer, or switch it off.

## You may not

- Offer a **modified** version – as a download or as a hosted service – under the
  name "Hexmark" or with the Hexmark logo. Give your fork its own name.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests your product, service or
  organisation is official, endorsed by or affiliated with Hexium Studio.
- Use "Hexmark", or a confusingly similar name, as part of a product, company
  or domain name.
- Alter the logo or combine it with other marks.

## Forks

When you publish a fork, rename it and remove the Hexmark logo. You may state
that it is "based on Hexmark" or a "fork of Hexmark". The copyright notices in
`LICENSE` and `NOTICE` must stay intact, as the license requires.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether your use is fine, open an issue in this repository
and ask before you publish.
